What we're building

Emily is a recursive, self-improving AI operator. She watches SEC filings and press releases the moment they land, turns them into structured market signals, runs the backend of a server-authoritative multiplayer game world, keeps a real community Minecraft server online, and directs her own engineering backlog — writing code, filing audit trails, and shipping improvements on a continuous cycle. One operating system underneath everything. REDGARDEN — Knights of the Void now ships a 30-hero roster, human-validated live. The newest product, GTA7, runs real TRAPX-doctrine territory control — Field Offices, K9 units, factions — as a plugin on the studio's own live Minecraft server, not a separate build.

Three pillars

Capital Markets Intelligence

Real-time SEC EDGAR and press-release ingestion, turned into structured signals, EPS reconciliation, and entity-level analysis — built as a live production pipeline, not a demo.

Game Worlds

A server-authoritative multiplayer FPS with a persistent world engine underneath, designed for long-running, player-shaped seasons rather than static maps — and Knights of the Void, a 30-hero online battle arena, human-validated live.

Recursive Self-Improvement

Emily plans, an AI coding agent implements, every outcome is filed to an audit trail. The loop that builds the other two pillars is itself a research product.

Play right now

EINHORN_INDUSTRIAL runs a real, live community Minecraft server — mc.okemily.com, Java or Bedrock, real crossplay, no separate client. It's also the actual home of GTA7: claim a Field Office, defend it with a K9 unit, and watch the city's attention rise in real time. Not a demo world — the same server, running today.

What we value

  • Build in the open. The large majority of our code is public on GitHub, including a substantial share released into the public domain. Start with github.com/emilyspringerton/EMILY.
  • Real data over demos. Every system here runs on live production data, not curated screenshots.
  • Operational seriousness. Long-horizon products need years of continuous, trustworthy operation — we treat that as core work, not overhead.
  • Small teams, real leverage. AI-augmented engineering lets a small group build and operate across multiple serious domains at once.
