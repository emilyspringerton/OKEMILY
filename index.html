The Moat: How Data Integrity Works

We don't trust client-side data, and we reject tracking cookies on principle. Every byte traversing the WOTAN engine is validated cryptographically at the infrastructure layer.

  • Zero-Cookie Privacy. We host no programmatic ad networks. The platform runs entirely on community support and direct contextual sponsorships.
  • Cryptographic Anti-Spoofing. We do not rely on local text log file validation. All match uploads require direct Battle.net OAuth Access Tokens. If the Account ID inside the raw client log doesn't match the token issued directly by Blizzard's security servers, the data is instantly trashed.
  • Statistical Anomaly Isolation. Decks do not enter public meta-tier directories until passing a minimum threshold of 2,000+ total matches across 50+ distinct, unique verified accounts, completely neutralizing “ghost deck” exploits.
